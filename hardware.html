Hardware

Pick your box, pick your buzzers.

Two boxes and two kinds of buzzers, with up to ten buzzers in a game. The BuzzerBox Compact plays with wireless buzzers only, the BuzzerBox Classic with wired or wireless ones. All of them run the same BuzzerBox software, bring their own WiFi and need no internet.
